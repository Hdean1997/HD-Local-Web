Common questions

Before we get started.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, services, service areas, contact details, any existing logo or colours, and any photos you'd like to use. If you don't have everything ready, we can work out what's actually needed first.

Does the £249 include a domain name?

The £249 starter price covers the website build. Domain names and any third-party services are separate, so you keep control of your own accounts and ongoing costs.

Will the website work properly on phones?

Yes. Every HD Local Web website is designed with mobile users in mind from the beginning.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. The starter package includes up to two rounds of reasonable changes after the first version is shown to you.

Can you build something bigger than one page?

Yes. £249 is the starter package. Larger or more complex websites can be quoted separately depending on what your business needs.
